# About

I am a Research Engineer in Natural Language Processing at EDF R&D [^1], where my work covers the following areas:

1. **Scientific monitoring.** I follow the state of the art in NLP through the literature, seminars and peer review for the main conferences of the field.
2. **Applied research and prototyping.** I design and develop proofs of concept to assess the maturity of emerging methods and their relevance to EDF's operational needs.
3. **Technology benchmarking.** I build evaluation protocols on internal data to support EDF business units in selecting commercial or open-source solutions.
4. **Generative AI expertise.** I contribute to EDF's internal generative AI community through tool development, workshops, training sessions and technical support.
5. **Research supervision.** I supervise PhD students and interns, primarily on encoder models and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and more recently on synthetic data generation.
